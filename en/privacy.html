MeeCal Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the MeeCal operations office ("we", "us") handles your information when you use the iOS app "MeeCal" (the "App"). It includes our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy for residents of Washington, Nevada and other U.S. states (Section 9).

1. Who we are

  • Controller: MeeCal operations office (Japan)
  • Contact: support@meecal.app
  • Availability: We offer the App in the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom and Australia. We do not offer it in the European Union.

2. Information we collect

We collect only what we need to provide the App.

(1) Account information

  • The user identifier and email address you share through Sign in with Apple or Google (if you choose "Hide My Email", a relay address)

(2) Profile information

  • Height, weight, age, sex, activity level, goals (the person you want to become, target weight, calorie target, etc.), country or region, and app settings (theme, reminder times, the AI partner's name, etc.)

(3) Health and food records — health data

  • Meal records (foods, calories, nutrients, time of meal)
  • Body composition records (weight, body fat percentage, BMI, etc.)
  • Personal notes saved from your conversations, such as food conversion rules and preferences

(4) Apple Health (HealthKit) data — health data, only if you turn it on

  • Read: weight, body fat percentage, height, biological sex, date of birth, and activity data used to estimate the calories you burn (steps and workouts)
  • Write: weight and body fat percentage you record in the App

(5) Conversations

  • Text you type in the App's chat, and your voice and its transcript when you use voice input

(6) Usage

  • How many times you use AI features (to keep the service stable and manage usage)

(7) Your consent records

  • When you agreed to or withdrew consent, the document version you agreed to, and the region the App used to show the consent screen

We do not collect the advertising identifier (IDFA), and we do not track you for advertising.

4. Your consent to process health data

The App only works by processing your health data (Section 2(3)–(4)) and sending part of it to our AI providers (Section 5). If you are outside Japan, the App asks for your explicit consent on the first screen before you start — separately to (a) processing your health data and (b) sending it to our AI providers — and records when you agreed and to which version of this policy.

  • Withdraw any time: My Page → "Health data consent" → "Withdraw consent". After you withdraw, the App stops processing your health data and you can no longer use it until you agree again. Withdrawal does not affect processing that happened before it.
  • Your records stay on our servers after you withdraw so that you can come back. To erase them, delete your account (Section 7). We do not delete them automatically, and we do not use them for anything else while your consent is withdrawn.

6. International transfers

We are based in Japan and store your data in Japan. The European Commission and the UK have recognized Japan as providing an adequate level of protection, so transfers from the EU or UK to Japan rely on those adequacy decisions. When the AI providers process data in the United States, the transfer relies on the providers' data processing terms, which include standard contractual clauses (and the UK International Data Transfer Addendum for UK data) or, where the provider is certified, the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and its UK Extension.

If you are in Canada or Australia, your information will be processed in Japan and the United States, whose laws may differ from yours. We remain responsible for your information and take reasonable steps to make sure our service providers protect it. Contact us if you would like more information about these transfers.

7. How long we keep your information, and deletion

  • We keep your information while your account exists.
  • You can delete your account and all records on our servers in the App: My Page → "Delete account". This cannot be undone. Deleted data may remain in encrypted backups for up to 30 days and is then overwritten.
  • Data the App wrote to Apple Health stays in your Health app. Delete it there if you wish.
  • Consent records are deleted together with your account.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • access your information and get a copy in a portable format,
  • correct it,
  • delete it (you can do this yourself in the App — Section 7),
  • withdraw consent (in the App — Section 4),
  • object to or restrict certain processing,
  • appeal our decision on your request, and
  • complain to your data protection authority (for example, the UK Information Commissioner's Office, or the data protection authority where you live in the EU).

To make a request, email support@meecal.app from the email address linked to your account. We will reply within 30 days (or sooner if the law of your country requires it). For a copy of your data, we will send your records in a machine-readable file (JSON or CSV). We will not charge you or treat you differently for using your rights.

9. Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy (U.S. residents)

This section applies to "consumer health data" under the Washington My Health My Data Act, Nevada SB 370, and similar U.S. state laws.

  • Categories we collect: body measurements (weight, body fat percentage, BMI, height), meals and nutrition you record, activity data from Apple Health (if enabled), and health-related goals and notes from your conversations.
  • Sources: you (what you type or say), and Apple Health if you turn it on.
  • Purposes: only to provide the App's features described in Section 3 — recording, calculating, AI responses and suggestions.
  • Sharing: only with the service providers in Section 5 (Supabase for storage, Google Gemini API and Anthropic Claude API for AI processing), which act on our behalf. We do not share consumer health data with any other third party or affiliate.
  • Sale: we do not sell consumer health data.
  • Consent: we collect and share consumer health data only with your consent, which you give on the first screen of the App. You can withdraw it any time (Section 4).
  • Your rights: you can confirm whether we collect, share or sell your consumer health data, get a list of the third parties we share it with and their contact information, access it, delete it, and withdraw consent. Use the in-app options (Sections 4 and 7) or email support@meecal.app. If we deny your request, you can appeal by replying to our decision; if your appeal is denied, you may contact your state Attorney General.
  • Geofencing: we do not use geofencing around health care facilities.

10. Security

  • All communication is encrypted with TLS.
  • Row-level security on our database prevents anyone other than you from accessing your records.
  • Your sign-in session is stored in your device's Keychain.

11. Apple Health (HealthKit)

  • We use HealthKit data only to provide the App's features, such as recording body composition and estimating your basal metabolism and calories burned.
  • We never use information from HealthKit for advertising or marketing, and never share it with data brokers or other third parties except the service providers in Section 5 needed to provide those features.
  • Connecting Apple Health is optional. You can change permissions any time in iOS Settings.

12. Children

The App is not a medical or diagnostic service and is not intended for children. You must be at least 16 years old (or the older age required in your country) to use it. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 16. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when laws or the App change. If we make important changes — especially to how we use or share health data — we will tell you in the App and ask for your consent again where required.
