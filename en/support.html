About MeeCal

MeeCal is an AI wellness app that records your meals and weight just by talking. Instead of filling in forms, you tell it what you ate, and the AI calculates calories and macros (protein, fat, carbs), manages your goals, and helps you with food choices.

  • Requires: iPhone with iOS 26.5 or later
  • Price: monthly or yearly auto-renewing subscription with a 7-day free trial. Prices are shown in the App Store in your local currency.

FAQ

How do I log a meal?
Just say or type something like "a protein shake and a banana this morning" in the chat on the Home screen. The AI picks out the foods, calories and nutrients and shows a confirmation card, which you can edit before saving.

Where do the calorie numbers come from?
For branded products, we prefer the manufacturer's published nutrition facts. For everyday foods, the values are estimates based on standard nutrition data. For foods you eat often, say "remember this" and it becomes your own conversion rule.

Can I use pounds and feet?
Yes. The App follows your iPhone's language and region settings for units and dates.

Do I have to connect Apple Health?
No, it's optional. Turn it on in My Page to import weight and body fat from smart scales that write to Apple Health, and to save the values you record in the App to Apple Health.

Why does the App ask for consent when I start?
MeeCal processes health data — your meals, weight and body composition — and sends part of it to AI providers (Google Gemini and Anthropic Claude) to answer you. Data protection laws in the UK, EU and several U.S. states require your explicit consent for this. You can withdraw it any time in My Page → "Health data consent". See our Privacy Policy.

How do I cancel my subscription?
Open iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions, and cancel any time. If you cancel during the free trial, you won't be charged.

How do I delete my account and data?
In the App, go to My Page → "Delete account". Your account and all records on our servers will be deleted. This cannot be undone.

Can I get a copy of my data?
Yes. Email support@meecal.app from the email address linked to your account, and we'll send your records in a machine-readable file.

Is the AI's advice medical advice?
No. MeeCal's calculations and suggestions are general wellness information and are not a substitute for diagnosis, treatment or medical advice from a doctor. If you have a medical condition or need to restrict your diet, please talk to a professional.
